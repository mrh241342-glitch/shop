Refund Policy

Last Updated: 1 January 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not, we offer a straightforward refund process.

Eligibility for Refunds

  • You may return most new, unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Items must be in original packaging with all accessories and tags intact.
  • Certain products (e.g., personal care items, software) may have different return policies – check the product page for details.

Non-Refundable Items

Gift cards, downloadable software, and perishable goods are generally non-refundable. Opened electronics may be subject to a restocking fee.

How to Initiate a Refund

  1. Log into your account and go to "My Orders".
  2. Select the order and click "Return Items".
  3. Follow the instructions to print a return label (if applicable).
  4. Pack the item securely and ship it to our returns address.

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Return shipping costs are your responsibility unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong item sent, defective product).

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our refund policy, please contact us at support@vismyth.com.
