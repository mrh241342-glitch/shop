Security Information

Last Updated: 1 January 2026

At VISMYTH, we take the security of your personal information seriously. We employ a range of technical and organisational measures to keep your data safe.

Data Encryption

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is protected with TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS). This ensures that sensitive information, such as your payment details and login credentials, is securely transmitted.

Payment Security

We do not store your credit/debit card details on our servers. Payments are processed through trusted third-party gateways that comply with PCI DSS standards.

Access Controls

Access to your personal information is restricted to authorised personnel only, and only to the extent necessary to provide our services. All employees are trained on data protection and privacy.

Monitoring and Incident Response

We continuously monitor our systems for potential vulnerabilities and attacks. In the event of a security breach, we have protocols in place to respond swiftly and notify affected users as required by law.

Your Responsibility

You play a vital role in protecting your own security. We recommend that you use a strong, unique password for your VISMYTH account and never share it with others. Also, ensure that you log out after each session, especially on shared devices.

Reporting a Security Issue

If you discover any potential security vulnerability, please report it to us immediately at security@vismyth.com. We will investigate and address the issue promptly.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the security practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy and security policies.
